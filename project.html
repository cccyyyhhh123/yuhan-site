Project ideas

Small things I could finish in a weekend — and one that took a month.

Things I might build

  • A campus guide for first-years: where things are, when the buses leave
  • A recipe converter for people who cook for one
  • A floor-plan sketch tool that turns a hand drawing into a 3D view
  • Ledger — a shared expense book, already built done

Who each one is for

Campus guide
For students who arrive in September and know nobody and nothing.
Recipe converter
For people who cook alone and keep halving recipes in their head.
Floor-plan tool
For anyone who has drawn a room on paper and wanted to walk through it.
Ledger
For a flat or a crew that splits money and keeps losing the list.

How I will build it

  1. Sketch the screens on paper — no code until the boxes make sense.
  2. Write the HTML first, with real words, so the page reads without CSS.
  3. Style it: one stylesheet, one set of colours, one type scale.
  4. Add the interaction last, and only the one that earns its place.

Ledger is live at cccyyyhhh123.github.io/ledger.
